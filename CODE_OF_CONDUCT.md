# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be harassment-free for everyone, whatever their age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith, and accept feedback gracefully.
- Be honest about results and limitations.

## Unacceptable behaviour

This includes harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, personal or political attacks, and
publishing others' private information. It also includes any conduct a reasonable person would consider
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer, [@nisaral](https://github.com/nisaral), or use GitHub's
"Report content" feature. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove contributions or
block participants who violate this code. The full enforcement guidelines are those of Contributor
Covenant 2.1.
